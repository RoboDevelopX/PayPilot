Rules

These rules decide what the assistant's proposals may do. The assistant can suggest a change, but every change it suggests waits for your approval; edits made here apply at once.

How a payout is decided

  • Blocked if the currency isn't allowed, or it would push today's or this month's payouts over the cap. Approval can't override a cap.
  • Needs your approval if it's above the auto-approve limit, or the recipient isn't on your allowlist.
  • Flagged as unusual (red card, extra confirmation) if the anomaly detector says it doesn't look like your past payouts.
  • Sent on its own otherwise.
The strictest rule wins. The ML signal can only add caution, never remove it.
