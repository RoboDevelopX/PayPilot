recent 20% of the synthetic data, which the models never saw while being fitted,
calibrated or thresholded. The data is invented (see `ml/MODEL_CARD.md`), so these

## Payout anomaly detector

Test window: 1225 payouts, 27 injected anomalies.
Threshold 0.6013 = the score that flags the top 3% of the
holdout window (a review budget, chosen without labels).

| detector | flagged | precision | recall | F1 | PR-AUC |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Isolation Forest** | 35 | 0.6286 | 0.8148 | 0.7097 | 0.8445 |
| baseline: generator rule | 98 | 0.2551 | 0.9259 | 0.4 | n/a |
| baseline: random | n/a | n/a | n/a | n/a | 0.022 |

ROC-AUC 0.9793; PR-AUC 95% bootstrap interval [0.7098, 0.9494].
With only 27 anomalies in the test window these numbers move a lot
between seeds; the interval shows how much.

| injected type | in test window | flagged |
|---|---|---|
| spike | 9 | 8 |
| new_large | 12 | 12 |
| burst | 6 | 2 |

**Reading it honestly:** a hand-written rule that mirrors how the anomalies were injected flags 98 payouts (F1 0.4) against the detector's 35 (F1 0.7097): knowing the recipe still over-flags busy recipients. The hardest type for the detector is `burst` (2 of 6 flagged).
In PayPilot a flag only escalates a payout for a careful human review; it never
blocks or sends anything.
